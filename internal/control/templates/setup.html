Looking for cameras…

Search a different network

A range no larger than a /24 (254 addresses) — that is one ordinary home or office network, and almost always holds every camera. The quickest way to name one is to paste a camera's address: 192.0.2.11 searches 192.0.2.0/24. Whatever you search is remembered in this browser and scanned automatically next time.

Add a camera by its address

For a camera on a username other than admin, or one the scan cannot reach. reostream connects and tells you what it is before anything is saved.
